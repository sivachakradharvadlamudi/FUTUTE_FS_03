## 1. Executive Summary & Business Background
Aroma Bistro & Cafe is an artisanal neighborhood cafe and bakery providing specialty organic coffees, fresh baked sourdough goods, and wholesome brunch meals. While the cafe enjoys strong walk-in traffic during peak hours, it previously lacked a dedicated digital presence and relied on third-party aggregators (with high 20–30% commission cuts) and unstructured phone inquiries.

## 2. Key Business Pain Points
1. **Aggregator Commission Drag:** Losing 20–30% margins on local takeout and pre-orders through external delivery platforms.
2. **Weekend Table Congestion & Customer Walkaways:** Customers arriving during peak brunch hours faced unpredictable 30+ minute wait times without a pre-booking system, resulting in lost sales.
3. **No Direct Customer Data Ownership:** Inability to notify regular patrons about weekend chef specials, discounts, or new seasonal coffee roasts.
4. **Poor Local Search Discoverability:** Lack of a structured, mobile-optimized menu for users searching "best coffee near me" on mobile browsers.

## 3. How This Website Solves These Problems
- **Interactive Responsive Digital Menu:** Fast loading, categorized menu (Coffee, Bakery, Mains) with transparent pricing and dish descriptions, allowing customers to decide and order faster.
- **Direct Online Table Reservation Engine:** Instant table booking system directly on the site, eliminating weekend table bottlenecks and reducing aggregator reliance.
- **Direct Customer Lead Capture:** Every reservation captures verified customer names and phone numbers stored securely in `localStorage` / backend database for direct SMS/WhatsApp promotional broadcasts.
- **Mobile-First SEO Architecture:** Built with clean semantic HTML5, fast styling, and localized schema-ready metadata (address, open hours, contact) to dominate local neighborhood searches.

## 4. Business ROI & Growth Impact
- **Cost Savings:** Zero commission fee on direct reservations and inquiries.
- **Capacity Optimization:** Distributes weekend footfall smoothly across reservation time slots (09:00 AM, 11:30 AM, 01:30 PM, 05:00 PM, 07:30 PM).
- **Projected Revenue Growth:** Estimated 20–30% increase in weekly bookings and higher repeat customer retention within the first 60 days of deployment.

I have built a custom, lightweight, and modern website specifically designed for your cafe. It features an interactive menu, clear opening hours, and a one-click table reservation system that collects customer contact info directly without charging you any commissions. I would love to walk you through the live demo and show how we can launch it to boost your weekend bookings."*
