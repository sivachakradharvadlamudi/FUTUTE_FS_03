About Our Cafe

Dedicated to the Art of Good Food & Meaningful Moments

Founded as a neighborhood sanctuary, Aroma Bistro brings people together over freshly roasted coffees and chef-crafted comforting meals. We partner directly with regional growers and bake every pastry from scratch every morning.

  • Specialty roasted Arabica and Robusta blends
  • Vegan and gluten-free dining options available
