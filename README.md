## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains the complete implementation of **Task 3: Local Business Website & Live Pitch Project** for the Future Interns Full Stack Web Development track.

The objective of this project is to build a responsive, conversion-focused website for a local business—**Aroma Bistro & Cafe**—and provide an accompanying strategic business pitch detailing how digital adoption drives footfall, eliminates third-party commission losses, and optimizes table turnaround.

---

## ☕ The Business: Aroma Bistro & Cafe
Aroma Bistro is a neighborhood cafe and bakery providing artisanal single-origin coffees, freshly baked sourdough pastries, and wholesome brunch meals. Prior to this project, the cafe had no independent digital menu or reservation system, relying primarily on foot traffic and third-party delivery apps charging 20–30% commissions.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- **Artisanal Café Branding:** Warm, welcoming visual palette aligned with specialty coffee culture.
- **Interactive Categorized Menu:** Filterable digital menu with category tabs (*All Items*, *Coffee & Tea*, *Bakery & Bites*, *Brunch & Mains*) displaying transparent pricing and dish descriptions.
- **Online Table Reservation System:** Dedicated booking form allowing guests to select date, time slot, and guest count with instant client-side validation and confirmation feedback.
- **Direct Lead & Reservation Storage:** Customer reservations are captured and stored in browser `localStorage` for customer relationship management.
- **Essential Business Information:** Prominently highlights opening hours, store location, and direct inquiry channels.
- **Mobile-First Responsive Layout:** Optimized for customers browsing on smartphones.

---

## 🎯 Executive Pitch Summary
*(Full strategic pitch document available in [`PITCH_EXPLANATION.md`](PITCH_EXPLANATION.md))*

1. **Pain Point:** Unpredictable weekend wait times leading to walkaways, and 20–30% profit erosion from third-party aggregators.
2. **Solution:** Direct, zero-commission online table booking and an accessible mobile menu.
3. **Projected ROI:** 20–30% increase in repeat customer retention and optimized peak-hour table allocation within 60 days of launch.
4. **Owner Pitch Script:**
   > *"Hello! I love the coffee and atmosphere here at Aroma Bistro. I built a custom website specifically tailored to your cafe featuring your full menu and a one-click table reservation system. It allows your guests to reserve tables in advance without charging you any commissions or third-party fees. I'd love to share the live demo with you!"*

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **HTML5:** Semantic architecture, accessible forms, and structured sections.
- **CSS3:** Custom variables, grid/flexbox layouts, responsive design, and modern card styling.
- **JavaScript (ES6+):** Category filtering logic, reservation form validation, and `localStorage` state management.
- **Font Awesome 6.4:** Clean icons for food items, store location, and contact details.
